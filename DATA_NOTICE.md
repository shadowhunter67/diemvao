# Data Notice

Official admission facts originate from their respective public publishers, such as ministry/regulator portals and school admission announcements.

UniScoreVN provides independently compiled and normalized runtime representations for the public app. The public repository may include generated runtime artifacts and official source URLs, but it does not publish the full private research pipeline, reviewer notes, source conflict logs, or normalized source-of-truth dataset.

Users should verify admission decisions against official announcements from the relevant authority or school.

This notice is not a custom restrictive data license and is not legal advice.
